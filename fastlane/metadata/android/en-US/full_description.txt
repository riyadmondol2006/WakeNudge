WakeNudge is a free, open-source alarm clock that makes it hard to go back to sleep. Built for heavy sleepers, shift workers, students and anyone who has slept through an alarm. No ads, no accounts, no subscriptions and no tracking. The only thing that goes online is an optional check for a newer version, which you can switch off.

HOW HARD?
Each alarm has one choice that sets everything else:
• Easy: swipe to stop, snooze allowed
• Normal: finish a mission to stop, snooze up to 3 times, keeps ringing until you do
• Hard: no snooze, the loudest the phone allows, checks you are still up a few minutes later
• No mercy: Hard, with two checks and the strictest limits
Change any detail under More options.

WAKE-UP MISSIONS
Eleven missions: math, memory patterns, typing, a QR code or barcode, an NFC tag, shaking, steps, squats, puzzles, a photo of a spot you chose, and Focus Tap, a quick attention game. Pick the difficulty, chain several, or get a different one each morning. A mission your phone can't do is replaced by a short backup mission, never skipped.

HARD TO WRIGGLE OUT OF
• Leave the alarm screen and it comes back in about a second, once you allow "Display over other apps"
• Restart the phone and the alarm rings again, at full volume with no snooze
• Turning the volume down while it rings doesn't stick
• An optional lock on changing the alarm in the hours before it rings, vacation mode included
• Optional device admin stops a half-asleep force stop or uninstall; you can turn it off again

ALWAYS AN EXIT
Every ring has an Emergency button that pauses it for a call. Alarms with a mission keep a way out without the mission: a 10-second hold, or on Hard and No mercy a run of tasks that opens after a few minutes. Power off isn't blocked, and Safe mode or a flat battery still beat it.

STILL AWAKE? CHECK
A few minutes after you dismiss, WakeNudge checks you are still up and rings again if not. On Hard and No mercy it asks for a round of Focus Tap or 30 steps, not a button.

A SOUND THAT WAKES YOU UP
• 20 tones from gentle to loud, including eight original music tracks, or your own music
• Volume ramp-up, Extra Loud mode and a sound that intensifies if ignored
• Strong vibration and spoken announcements
• Sunrise mode, a backup alarm and a torch that can flash when you ignore it
• Hearing-safety limit on headphones, and your alarm volume put back afterwards

SLEEP, HABITS AND YOUR WEEK
• Report tab: average wake-up time and time to get up, week by week
• Sleep tracking with the phone on your bed. Stages are estimated from movement and sound, not measured. Snore recording is optional and off by default, stays on your phone and is deleted after 7 days
• Sleep sounds: rain, ocean, fan, noise and more, fading out on a timer. Any alarm stops them
• Habits with streaks and gentle reminders

PLAN YOUR DAYS
Unlimited alarms with repeat days, groups like Work and Weekend, skip next ring, vacation mode, holiday and calendar exceptions, bedtime reminders and a sleep planner. Plus a timer, stopwatch, world clock, widgets and a Quick Settings tile.

ALARM HEALTH
A checkup shows whether notifications, full-screen alerts, exact alarms, battery or Do Not Disturb could stop an alarm, how to fix each, and rings a real test alarm. Each evening it checks the next alarm and tells you only if something is wrong.

PRIVACY AND PRICE
No account, no ads, no analytics. Alarms, history, habits and sleep data stay on your phone. Back up alarms, settings and habits to a file you choose; nothing is uploaded. Camera, microphone and calendar are asked for only when you use the feature that needs them. Everything is free, and features never go away.

A NOTE ON RELIABILITY
No app can ring a phone that is switched off, and some manufacturers restrict background apps. WakeNudge has been tested on a Pixel; Alarm health tells you what on your phone could get in the way.
