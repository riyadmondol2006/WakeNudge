# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Welcome behaviour includes being kind and patient, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive
feedback gracefully, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Reasons are given when it is safe to
do so.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone is officially
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

To report a problem privately, use the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the
report with "Code of Conduct" so it is not mistaken for a security issue
([link](https://github.com/riyadmondol2006/WakeNudge/security/advisories/new)). For something in a public thread you can
also use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. All reports are handled promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's ladder: a private **correction** for unclear or unwelcome behaviour, a
**warning** with consequences for continued behaviour, a **temporary ban** from interaction for serious violations, and a
**permanent ban** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards individuals or groups.
